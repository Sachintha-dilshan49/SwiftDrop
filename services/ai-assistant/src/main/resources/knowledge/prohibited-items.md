# Prohibited items

## Items you cannot send
SwiftDrop does not carry: illegal drugs and narcotics; weapons, ammunition and explosives, including
fireworks; flammable liquids and gases such as petrol, kerosene, gas cylinders and aerosols;
corrosive or toxic chemicals; live animals; cash, cheques and bearer documents; and anything
illegal to own or send in Sri Lanka.

## Liquids, perfume and batteries
Perfume and alcohol-based sprays are flammable and are not accepted. Sealed non-flammable liquids
are accepted if they are packed so they cannot leak. Loose lithium batteries are not accepted;
batteries fitted inside a device are accepted.

## Food and perishables
Packaged, non-perishable food is accepted. Fresh or frozen food is sent at your own risk, because
SwiftDrop does not offer refrigerated delivery.

## Valuables
Jewellery, phones and other valuables can be sent, but pack them well. SwiftDrop does not offer
insurance in this version.

## If you are not sure
Contact SwiftDrop support before booking. Couriers may refuse a parcel that looks unsafe.
