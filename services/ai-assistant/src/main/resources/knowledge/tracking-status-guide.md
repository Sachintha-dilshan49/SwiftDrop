# Tracking and parcel statuses

## How to track a parcel
Every parcel gets a tracking number such as SD-7K3Q-9P2M. Anyone with the tracking number can follow
the parcel on the public tracking page without logging in. The page updates live while it is open.
Signed-in customers also get an email on every status change, and the recipient gets an SMS.

## CREATED
The booking is confirmed and the parcel is waiting to be picked up.

## PICKED_UP
A courier has collected the parcel from the pickup address.

## IN_TRANSIT
The parcel is on its way between hubs or zones.

## OUT_FOR_DELIVERY
A courier has the parcel and is delivering it today.

## DELIVERED
The parcel was handed over. A proof-of-delivery photo may be recorded. This is a final status.

## FAILED
The courier could not deliver the parcel and recorded a reason, for example nobody was available.
FAILED can only follow OUT_FOR_DELIVERY and is a final status. See the failed delivery article.

## Order of statuses
Statuses only move forward: CREATED, PICKED_UP, IN_TRANSIT, OUT_FOR_DELIVERY, then DELIVERED or FAILED.
A parcel never goes back to an earlier status.
