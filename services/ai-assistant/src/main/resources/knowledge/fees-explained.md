# How delivery fees are calculated

## The formula
Every fee is calculated by SwiftDrop's pricing service, never by the app or a merchant.
fee = route base fee + extra weight + size surcharge + express surcharge − promo discount.
All amounts are in Sri Lankan rupees (LKR). The fee never goes below 0.

## Route base fee
The base fee depends on the pickup and drop-off zones.
Same zone: LKR 300. Neighbouring zones in the same group: LKR 350.
Colombo suburbs: LKR 400. Inter-city (either end outside the Colombo area): LKR 650.

## Weight
The first kilogram is included. Each extra kilogram costs LKR 40, rounded up to the next whole kilogram.
The maximum weight for one parcel is 40 kg.

## Size surcharge
SMALL: no surcharge (up to 5 kg). MEDIUM: LKR 100 (up to 10 kg).
LARGE: LKR 250 (up to 20 kg). EXTRA_LARGE: LKR 500 (up to 40 kg).

## Delivery speed
EXPRESS (within 3 hours): LKR 100 extra. STANDARD (same day by 8 PM): no extra charge.
NEXT_DAY: no extra charge.

## Promo codes
A promo code takes a fixed amount off, for example DROP100 takes LKR 100 off.
Enter the code when you book; the discount is shown before you confirm.

## Estimate and final fee
The fee estimate shows the expected price. When you confirm a booking, SwiftDrop calculates the
binding fee again and saves it with its breakdown, so your receipt never changes later.

## Worked example
Colombo South to Colombo Central, 1.2 kg, SMALL, EXPRESS with promo DROP100:
base 350 + extra weight 40 + size 0 + express 100 = 490, minus 100 promo = LKR 390.
