# Delivery times and zones

## Where SwiftDrop delivers
SwiftDrop delivers in Colombo and nearby cities. Addresses are grouped into zones, and the pickup
and drop-off zones decide the route base fee. The zone list in the app is always the current one.

## Delivery speeds
EXPRESS: delivered within 3 hours of pickup.
STANDARD: delivered the same day by 8 PM.
NEXT_DAY: delivered the next day.

## Following the delivery
Use the tracking number to follow the parcel live. When the status is OUT_FOR_DELIVERY, the courier
is delivering it that day.
