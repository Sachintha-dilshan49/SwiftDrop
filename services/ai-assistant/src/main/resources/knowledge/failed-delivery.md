# When a delivery fails

## What FAILED means
If the courier cannot deliver the parcel, they mark it FAILED and record a reason, for example the
recipient was not available or the address could not be found. FAILED is a final status.

## What to do next
Check the reason on the tracking page. Contact SwiftDrop support to arrange what happens to the
parcel. The assistant cannot rebook or change a delivery.

## Avoiding failed deliveries
Give a complete address and the recipient's correct phone number when you book, so the courier can
call before arriving. Choose a delivery speed that suits when someone will be available.
