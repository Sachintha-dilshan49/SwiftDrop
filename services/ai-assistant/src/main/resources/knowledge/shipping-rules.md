# Parcel sizes, weight and packaging

## Sizes and weight limits
SMALL: up to 5 kg. MEDIUM: up to 10 kg. LARGE: up to 20 kg. EXTRA_LARGE: up to 40 kg.
Parcels heavier than 40 kg cannot be sent with SwiftDrop.

## Choosing the right size
Choose the size that matches both the weight and the bulk of the parcel. If the courier finds that
a parcel is larger or heavier than booked, contact SwiftDrop support.

## Packaging
Pack items so they cannot move inside the box and seal the parcel securely.
Write the tracking number on the parcel or attach the label shown after booking.
Fragile items need extra padding; SwiftDrop does not offer a separate fragile service.

## What you need to book
Pickup and drop-off address and zone, the recipient's name and phone number, size, weight,
delivery speed and an optional promo code. You see the fee before you confirm.
